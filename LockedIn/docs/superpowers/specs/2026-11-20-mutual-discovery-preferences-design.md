# Mutual matching for the first Discovery pilot

## Intent and approaches

Discovery should introduce people who are open to meeting each other, not just people in the same area. Start with a short required setup and a strict match in both directions. Leave the existing interest-based order, local-market rules, chats, and shared spaces in place.

- **Rank by preferences:** Less setup friction and more visible profiles in a small pool, but people still appear to someone who explicitly excluded them. Not selected.
- **Require a mutual fit:** Use private, explicit choices as eligibility rules, then use today's ranking to order the eligible profiles. A metro can honestly have few or no results. Selected for the first pilot.

Reporting improvements are the next separate project before the pilot opens. This project does not add identity verification, a public compatibility score, analytics, new notifications, or a redesigned inbox.

## Setup and member controls

- To browse or be shown in Discovery, new and existing members complete the same short setup: date of birth, how they identify for matching, the genders they are open to meeting, and their preferred adult age range. Offer explicit **Open to all genders** and **Any adult age** choices; do not preselect them or infer any choice from a name, photo, profile, or prior chat.
- Proposed first-release gender options for self-description: **Woman**, **Man**, **Nonbinary**, and **Another gender**. A person chooses their own category; do not ask for sex at birth or a separate trans-status field. Meeting preferences are multi-select over the same choices or explicitly all. People who do not want to provide this information can skip Discovery and keep using direct invites and existing conversations.
- Use date of birth to compute completed years from a date-only value and the current UTC calendar date; do not use a stored age that goes stale. A February 29 birthday advances on March 1 in a non-leap year. Reject invalid, future, and under-18 entries, keep the existing adult attestation, and do not call either check age or identity verification. Require deliberate confirmation for birthday corrections and allow one successful correction per rolling 30 days. Do not store a history of old birthdays.
- Save the member's settings privately. A valid save completes the matching setup, not the public-profile step. Existing published, unpaused profiles can re-enter Discovery after explicitly completing the settings; never publish a draft, unpause someone, or resume a mutually locked account automatically. Keep the local location check and the other existing gates. Members can edit their choices or remove their matching setup; removal is always allowed and erases the matching values, including date of birth, without changing the public snapshot or old connections. Re-enrolling inside 30 days after removal uses the same correction allowance. Keep only the minimum timestamps needed to enforce that allowance until they expire, so removal cannot reset it; retain no removed values and clear the rate-limit metadata on account deletion.

## Mutual eligibility and privacy

- Compare in both directions: each person's chosen matching gender must be accepted by the other, and each person's current age must be inside the other's inclusive adult range. Apply this only after both have completed setup, with all existing safety, availability, intent, location and existing-connection rules still in force. Do not widen anyone's ranges, search elsewhere, or substitute one-sided results when a pool is empty.
- With the feature on, enforce the check on city/metro and distance Discovery lists, individual unconnected profile reads (including photo delivery), new sends, and pending Discovery acceptance. Use current settings under the existing pair protections for contact decisions. A stale profile URL or invitation cannot bypass the check. Pending invites remain visible and can be declined, withdrawn or reported; if the pair no longer qualifies, accepting is unavailable with neutral wording. Do not expose which person or which preference prevented a match.
- Existing accepted Discovery conversations, direct invitations, shared-space chats, date plans, moderation evidence and block/report controls keep their existing permissions and behavior. Never add matching rules to the predicate used to decide whether an established chat is writable. Existing safety restrictions still take precedence.
- Keep the matching record separate from the published profile and other shared data. Exact birthday, derived age, matching gender and the member's choices are not displayed to others in this release or written into analytics, logs, notification copy, public profile or safety evidence. Only their owner can retrieve their values. As with any reciprocal filter, visibility can imply compatibility; do not promise that no inference is possible.
- Use the existing database-filtered pagination, not browser-side filtering or a cap applied to unfiltered pages. A fresh settings edit should restart the viewer's search; old cursors and cached or pending results must still be checked against current eligibility. Keep normal empty states neutral and avoid target-specific counts or preference explanations.

## Clean start, rollout, and verification

- The clean start is a new setup gate, not a data purge. Add the private matching record without guessing or backfilling anyone's settings. Once the server gate is enabled, incomplete profiles are out of the new Discovery pool until they choose to return. Preserve accounts, photos, profile content, chats, invitations, plans, reports, and shared spaces. If the owner separately requests removal of disposable test data, produce a read-only inventory of exact records and agree on recoverability and scope before running a cleanup. No cleanup is needed to ship the matching experience.
- Introduce an API-side flag, off by default, and an additive migration. Apply the migration and compatible API before the web build. Test both flag states on a disposable environment; do not assume the Git staging branch is a separate database. Do not enable broad access or select a launch metro in this project. Turning off the matching flag restores previous Discovery eligibility without deleting settings or changing the access mode. Hide new setup and stop accepting new matching values while off; members who already saved settings can still remove them.
- Verify mutual versus one-way fits; inclusive age edges and birthdays (including leap-day births); midnight/time-zone consistency; strict setup and edit validation; paused, opted-out and locked-in members; city/metro and distance pagination; stale links and preferences changed between send and acceptance; own-account privacy and projection boundaries; and unchanged existing chats, shared spaces, reports, and direct-invite behavior.

The builder chooses the exact schema, query helpers, and editing surface. This design does not authorize product implementation, operator data deletion, flag changes, or rollout.
