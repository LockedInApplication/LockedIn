# Homepage artwork

The homepage uses the original Blue Hour scene in `apps/web/public/home/`: two fictional adults talking outside a record shop after rain. It was created with the built-in image generator and exported to WebP in two landscape sizes and a tighter phone crop. It does not depict LockedIn members or use artwork from the reference site.

The private-space section uses `apps/web/public/home/editorial-coffee.webp`, an optimized crop of the earlier coffee-date scene. It depicts fictional people, not a testimonial or LockedIn members.

The Discovery phone preview uses `apps/web/public/home/discovery-preview-maya.webp`, cropped from that same scene. Its profile and the Atlas chapter in the Together phone are fictional examples; the previews never load member data.

The homepage serves Source Serif 4 and Roboto locally. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are in `apps/web/public/fonts/source-serif-4-OFL.txt` and `apps/web/public/fonts/roboto-OFL.txt`.

## Earlier coffee-date artwork

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait editorial hero photo for LockedIn, an intentional dating and relationship website. Create a candid 4:5 film photograph of two young adult Black people on an unhurried first coffee date, seated facing each other in profile on a softly sunlit city café terrace. One is mid-laugh, both relaxed and present; two small ceramic cups on the table, the moment feels unposed. Medium-wide composition with both adults around the central middle third, a dark muted plum or rust sweater, cream shirt, green foliage and warm cream architecture, late afternoon golden light. Subtle 35mm grain, natural texture, no beauty retouching, a little atmosphere. Leave the lower left relatively calm for a small website caption overlay, and keep the subjects comfortably inside a crop. No lettering, no signage, no logo, no watermark, no phone or interface.
