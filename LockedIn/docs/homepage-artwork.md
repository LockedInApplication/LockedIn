Create a candid 4:5 film photograph of two young adult Black people on an unhurried first coffee date, seated facing each other in profile on a softly sunlit city café terrace. One is mid-laugh, both relaxed and present; two small ceramic cups on the table, the moment feels unposed. Medium-wide composition with both adults around the central middle third, a dark muted plum or rust sweater, cream shirt, green foliage and warm cream architecture, late afternoon golden light. Subtle 35mm grain, natural texture, no beauty retouching, a little atmosphere. Leave the lower left relatively calm for a small website caption overlay, and keep the subjects comfortably inside a crop. No lettering, no signage, no logo, no watermark, no phone or interface.
