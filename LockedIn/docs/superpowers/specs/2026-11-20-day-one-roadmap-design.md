# Our next steps, from day one

## What changes

Keep **Together → Our next steps** as the one Roadmap. Make it useful the moment a two-person shared space opens, whether the pair locked in through Discovery or got there through a direct invitation. Neither person needs to complete a guided check-in. Inbox remains the place to chat; the Roadmap does not post planning activity into the transcript.

The first screen asks, “What would you like to do together?” with one primary action, **Suggest a plan**. A small form offers starter ideas such as “Cook together,” “Try a new spot,” and “Make time to talk,” plus a custom title. Selecting an idea only fills in the title. An optional day and note can be added before tapping **Send suggestion**. The day on a simple plan is for planning, not a reservation or a revisit deadline. People wanting venue search and an exact time can open the existing date planner in Inbox.

## The shared list

- **Needs a reply:** Put incoming suggestions first. The recipient can **Accept**, **Suggest a change**, or **Pass**. Show outgoing suggestions as waiting for the other person, with **Withdraw** available.
- **Coming up:** Show mutually accepted plans with future days in date order, followed by undated plans and plans whose day has passed but that are still open. A future, confirmed date from the pair’s authorized chat appears as a link to its original date sheet. Canceling it in the planner removes it from Coming up. The Roadmap does not schedule, copy, or edit these date plans.
- **Past plans:** Collapse resolved simple plans and existing resolved steps by default. A plan never completes automatically. For a dated plan, **Mark done** becomes available at the start of its scheduled day in the time zone used to suggest that day. Plans made without a time zone wait until the day has started everywhere. Undated plans can be marked done once accepted. Existing date history remains in Inbox.

## How agreement works

Each suggestion belongs to the shared space and has one current version. A counter updates the same suggestion, shows who proposed the revision, and waits for the other person to accept that exact version. Neither person can accept their own proposal. Passing or withdrawing ends the pending suggestion without deleting it. Either person can mark an eligible plan done or cancel an accepted plan. Show who took the action without implying both people verified the outcome. Either person can choose **Undo done** in Past plans to restore the agreed plan to Coming up without a new acceptance. Other resolved statuses cannot be reopened. Replanning after agreement requires a new suggestion rather than silently changing the old agreement.

Only current members of the active shared space may read or change this list. Reuse the app’s existing blocking and shared-space rules; enforce access and the expected plan version when applying changes. If someone responds to an outdated version, show the current suggestion and ask them to review it before responding again. A retry should not create a second suggestion, decision, or alert.

Send one quiet in-app alert to the member who needs to reply for each new proposal or counter, using the existing notification surface and respecting preferences. The alert links to the relevant suggestion and does not contain its title or note. Resolve that alert when the proposal is answered, superseded, or withdrawn. Accepted and resolved states update in the Roadmap. Add no new phone alerts, emails, or repeated reminders for simple plans.

## How it fits the existing app

Plan access and the Roadmap list must work without a check-in session. Keep existing check-in steps, agreements, revisit dates, and reflections intact and continue to display them in the same Roadmap, with their existing actions and labels. The check-in’s existing next-step action and consent rules remain; they are one way to add a step, not a condition for using Together. Existing private notes do not become visible to partners. Preserve any intentional reveal rules for existing check-ins; new simple plans do not introduce a required reflection or any automatic sharing.

Keep the existing date planner authoritative for place, time, confirmation, and cancellation. Only project future confirmed dates from a chat that belongs to this shared space and is still accessible to the viewer, using the planner’s permissions and visibility settings. A Roadmap link opens the exact original plan, not whatever plan happens to be current. If the date feed is unavailable, the rest of the Roadmap still works and the UI indicates that dates could not load.

Build the day-one list and agreement flow first, then add the confirmed-date links against the same Coming up section. No second tab, calendar sync, recurring goals, streaks, memory collection, or new post-date check-ins in this release.

## Release checks

- With two accounts, test both a mutual Discovery lock-in and an accepted direct invite. In each case, propose a plan before any check-in, counter, accept, and find the agreed plan without disturbing chat.
- Test both members’ views, pass and withdraw, stale versions and concurrent responses, exact retries, and access from an unrelated account or after blocking or ending the shared space.
- Verify completion is blocked before the scheduled day and appears when it starts. Confirm either member can undo completion without changing the agreement, including for an existing plan marked done early.
- Verify existing check-in agreements and reflections are still available and private notes stay private. Check that quiet alerts reach only the member expected to respond, once per version, and clear when no action is needed.
- Confirm an authorized scheduled date appears only once, opens the original date sheet, and leaves Coming up after cancellation or when its scheduled time passes. Check the Roadmap remains usable without date planning enabled.
- Verify the empty, pending, upcoming, and past states on mobile and desktop, including keyboard access and a clear return path to Inbox.
